CINEMATIC VISUAL EFFECTS

BEYOND THE IMAGINATION.

Visual Effects (VFX) transform the possibilities of filmmaking by combining live-action footage, computer-generated imagery and digital techniques. AUTOTUBE CREW STUDIO focuses on cinematic visual effects and digital production capabilities for films, advertisements, entertainment and creative projects. Our approach brings together visual storytelling, technical precision and creative direction to develop compelling screen experiences.

OUR VFX CAPABILITIES

CREATING THE UNSEEN.

01 / COMPOSITING

Digital Compositing

Combining multiple visual elements, live-action footage and digital assets into seamless cinematic compositions.

02 / CGI INTEGRATION

CGI Integration

Integrating computer-generated objects, characters and environments into live-action scenes.

03 / ENVIRONMENTS

Digital Environment Creation

Developing digital backgrounds, virtual locations, landscapes and environment extensions for cinematic scenes.

04 / ROTOSCOPING

Rotoscoping & Cleanup

Chroma Key & Screen Replacement

Replacing green screen backgrounds and integrating subjects into digitally created environments.
